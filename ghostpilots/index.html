Ghost Pilots

Turn & shoot arcade action for iPhone and iPad.

Ghost Pilots is an arcade game with a simple layout: turn and shoot. Fly through waves of enemies, take on the Turret Boss, chase a high score in Endless mode, or go head to head with friends online.

Modes

  • Turret Boss — survive the boss fight and earn Polynite.
  • Endless — keep flying as long as you can and climb the Game Center leaderboard.
  • Online — battle your friends in real time.

Ghost Pilots Support

Happy to help with any issue, question, or feedback about Ghost Pilots. Email hello@dhrooby.com and you'll usually get a reply within 2–3 days.

Common questions

My high score didn't save to the leaderboard. Scores post to Game Center, so sign in to Game Center in the Settings app and play a round to sync your best score.

Online mode won't connect. Online play needs an internet connection on both devices. If a match won't start, close the app fully and reopen it.

Reporting a bug. Include your device model and iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

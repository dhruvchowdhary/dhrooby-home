Privacy Policy

Ghost Pilots · Last updated October 13, 2026

This policy explains what Ghost Pilots collects and why. Ghost Pilots does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, or contacts.

What is collected

  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • How you use the app, such as game sessions, modes played, and scores
  • Advertising data, such as ads shown and tapped
  • Crash and performance data

How it's used

This data is used to run the game's features, show ads, measure how the app performs, and fix crashes and bugs.

Services used

  • Google AdMob serves the ads in the app and may use the advertising identifier to personalize them. Learn more
  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Realtime Database) handles usage analytics and online multiplayer. Learn more
  • Apple Game Center stores leaderboard scores if you are signed in. Learn more

Your choices

iOS asks for your permission before the app can track you across other apps and websites, and you can change that at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can also turn off personalized ads under Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising. Deleting the app stops any further collection.

Children

Ghost Pilots is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
